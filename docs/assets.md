# Asset Sources

The opening hero uses the locally bundled electronics photo as an illustrative background, not a photograph of the portfolio owner's work. Other stock photos remain unused legacy demo assets. The project catalog, case studies, about page, and social metadata do not use these stock photos. Project directories in `public/images/projects/<slug>/` contain supplied JPG images alongside remaining labeled PNG placeholders. Placeholders are generated by `scripts/generate-photo-placeholders.mjs`; existing images are never overwritten. Main images fit inside wide frames with white padding rather than cropping. The Cyr wheel uses four JPGs and reuses its main JPG as the video-area poster. Its supplied `video-poster.mp4` is copied unchanged to `public/videos/cyr-wheel/demo.mp4` for native video playback; the original remains intact. No photos or fonts are requested from third-party hosts by the deployed website.
