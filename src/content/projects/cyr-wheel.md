## The project

A Cyr wheel is a circular apparatus used for movement with a performer inside the ring. Creating one brings the design into direct contact with the person using it: shape, surface finish, balance, and handling all matter.

This project follows my creation of a custom wheel, from the physical build to the finished object. Its materials, dimensions, and fabrication methods remain to be documented alongside the build photographs.

## Design and fabrication

The build is as much about a continuous, predictable physical shape as it is about the individual parts. Roundness, alignment, and the transitions between construction details affect how the finished wheel feels and moves.

The photographs will document the finished wheel, an early build stage, the fabrication process, and a close-up construction detail. No particular material, joining process, or surface treatment is assumed here until the actual build details are recorded.

## Engineering considerations

- **Geometry and alignment:** a consistent ring and smooth transitions matter for predictable handling.
- **Structural details:** any joints and connections need to be understood in the context of the actual material and construction method.
- **Contact surfaces:** the finish affects grip, contact with the floor, and how the wheel is handled.
- **Physical testing:** an apparatus used with a person requires an appropriate inspection and assessment; photographs or a demonstration are not a substitute for structural verification.

## The finished wheel

The finished-wheel photographs and movement video will complete the build record. Useful details to add include the chosen dimensions and materials, fabrication decisions, final finish, and observations from actual use. No load rating or safety certification is claimed.
